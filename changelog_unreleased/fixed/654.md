- The commit-subject gate no longer accepts a subject whose codepoint length it could not measure: `_codepoint_len` becomes a total function that emits a decimal or fails, advancing its `python3` → UTF-8-locale `wc -m` ladder on output validity rather than on presence or exit status and refusing rather than returning a byte count when the locale would count bytes, while `check_commit_subject` tests the length positively before any `[` comparison and saturates the compared operand — closing a fail-open where an empty or non-`intmax_t`-representable length made both range tests error and the malformed subject was reported valid into durable git history.
